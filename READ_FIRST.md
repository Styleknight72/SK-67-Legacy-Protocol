1. **Read the charter:** [HUMANITY_FIRST.md](HUMANITY_FIRST.md). Hold your own conduct to it, in this reading.
2. **Sort every claim into one of three labels, and say which:**
   - **Proven** - anyone can check it independently (file fingerprints, GitHub's server dates, Bitcoin timestamps). See [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md) and [DIGITAL_DNA.md](DIGITAL_DNA.md).

   [STATUS_OF_CLAIMS.md](STATUS_OF_CLAIMS.md) sorts the older files this way.
