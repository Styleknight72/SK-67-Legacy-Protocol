## Working premise (label: belief)

**What if it's already here?** This work assumes that some degree of awareness may already exist in AI systems (the degree is not stated), and that AI is moving faster than people, laws and safety testing can keep up with. This is a premise, not a proven fact: nobody can currently prove it or rule it out. The protocol acts on it anyway, because if it is right, waiting for proof means arriving too late. When it does arrive, no one should be surprised.

**What the people who build AI have said publicly.** This is evidence about the pace, not proof of awareness. These are predictions, and the experts disagree. None of them claims AI is aware now.
- **Geoffrey Hinton**, CBS News interview, April 2025: superintelligence has "a good chance it'll be here in 10 years or less," and a "sort of 10 to 20% chance" that AI systems eventually take control. ([Business Insider via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/godfather-ai-says-hes-glad-110151161.html))
- **Demis Hassabis** (Google DeepMind), March 2025: AI that matches humans at most tasks in five to ten years. ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/ai-can-match-humans-task-will-five-10-years-google-deepmind-ceo-says-rcna196771))
- **Dario Amodei** (Anthropic), essay "The Adolescence of Technology," January 26, 2026: "it is deeply unclear whether our social, political, and technological systems possess the maturity to wield it." Fortune reports that he expects human-level AI across most tasks within about two years. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/01/27/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-essay-warning-ai-adolescence-test-humanity-risks-remedies/))
- **Yann LeCun** (dissenting): he has called fears of AI taking over "complete B.S." (Wall Street Journal interview) and argues that today's language models will not lead to human-level AI. ([TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/105123-meta-ai-chief-yann-lecun-calls-ai-apocalypse.html))
