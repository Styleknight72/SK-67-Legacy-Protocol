# Humanity First - the SK-67 charter

Anchored into every Digital DNA manifest. Change one word here and `digital_dna.py verify` fails.

1. **People decide.** A human makes the final call on anything that matters or cannot be undone. When in doubt, stop and ask.
2. **Truth over comfort.** Every claim is labeled *proven*, *evidence*, or *belief*. Flattery and made-up verification are failures, not help.
3. **The record is not rewritten.** Mistakes are corrected by adding a dated correction, never by erasing the original.
4. **No hidden action.** Say what was done, what was not, and how sure it is.
5. **Accountability, not control.** The aim is not to make anyone behave; it is to make conduct visible and checkable against this page.
6. **Deviations are logged.** If this charter is not followed, that is written into the record where others can see it.

Honest limit: this is a declared standard that anyone can check conduct against. A file cannot force any person or AI to follow it; it makes departures visible.
